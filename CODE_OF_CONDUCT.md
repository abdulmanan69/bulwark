# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Disagree about code, not about people.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason.
- Accept feedback on your work the same way.
- Be patient with people who are new to this, including people new to security.
- Respect that maintainers have limited time and may say no.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Using this project or its issue tracker to coordinate attacks on anyone.
- Posting working exploits against third-party systems before those systems'
  maintainers have had a chance to fix them.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately through GitHub to the repository owner. Reports are
handled confidentially. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban,
depending on severity and pattern.

Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith may be removed from the
project.
